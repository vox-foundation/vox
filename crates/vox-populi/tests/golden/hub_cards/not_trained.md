# Vox MENS — Qwen3-32B adapter

32B is [not trained](https://voxlang.org/reference/mens-models/#state-legend): `train-record.json` says “above non-fitting 4B (D-06)”.

![Qwen3-32B scorecard](assets/scorecard.svg)

## Why this size

The largest rung, planned for machines at the top of the memory range and not available yet. It is meant for the hardest cross-file work once a smaller size shows the approach holds. Until it ships, pick a smaller size.

This card is generated by `vox mens hub sync`; the [ladder index](https://huggingface.co/ns/vox-mens-index) lists the other sizes and [how the cards are made](https://voxlang.org/reference/mens-models/#how-the-cards-are-made) is on the docs page.
