## Root Causes Diagnosed

1. **Button stair-stepping & uneven sibling spacing:** Starlight's `.sl-markdown-content :not(a, strong, em, del, span, input, code, br) + :not(..., :where(.not-content *))` rule injected `margin-top: 1rem` (`16px`) onto 2nd+ `<button>` children inside `VoxPlayground.astro` and `VoxLeaderboardArena.astro`, and onto 2nd+ cards inside `.vox-progressive-tiers` and `.vox-showcase-grid`.
2. **Programmatic gradient contrast ambiguity:** Linear-gradient CTA buttons left `background-color` at `transparent`, causing computed-style contrast audits (`getComputedStyle(el).backgroundColor`) to read parent backgrounds (`1.01:1`).
3. **Uneven row heights & buried GUI showcase:** Onboarding cards, `<CardGrid>` cards, and the bottom 10-card Axis showcase stack differed in bullet count / vertical rhythm and buried the GUI tour deep below `Why Vox`.
4. **Tutorial capture staleness:** `vox-search-arcane.webp`, `discovery-arcane.webp`, and `scientia-arcane.webp` previously carried toast overlays, stale `32-surface` labels, or tightly clipped `ARCHIVE DEPOSIT STATUS` panes.
