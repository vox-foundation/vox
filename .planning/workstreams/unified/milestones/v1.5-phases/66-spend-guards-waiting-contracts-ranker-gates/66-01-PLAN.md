# Phase 66 Plan 01: Effect-Enforced Spend/Write/ExternalApi Guards & `<WaitingNotice>` View Contracts (`RANK-01..02`)

## Objective
Extend Vox's algebraic effect checker (`uses { ... }` in `crates/vox-compiler/`) with `Spend`, `Write`, and `ExternalApi` effects so `query` functions are statically guaranteed never to incur paid API/scrape spend or production writes (`RANK-01`), and add `when waiting(reason, resume_at, action_href)` → `<WaitingNotice>` (`RANK-02`, `A1-04`) in `crates/vox-codegen/src/web_ir/async_state.rs`.

## Tasks
1. **`Spend`, `Write`, and `ExternalApi` Algebraic Effects (`RANK-01`)**:
   - Extend `EffectAnnotation` (`crates/vox-ast/src/decl/effect.rs`), `HirEffectKind` (`crates/vox-compiler/src/hir/nodes/effect.rs`), and `HirCapability` (`crates/vox-compiler/src/hir/nodes/decl.rs`) with `Spend`, `Write`, and `ExternalApi`.
   - Enforce `E_QUERY_FORBIDDEN_EFFECT` in `crates/vox-compiler/src/typeck/effect_check.rs` when a `query` endpoint declares or calls functions requiring `Spend`, `Write`, or `ExternalApi`.
2. **`<WaitingNotice>` View Contract (`RANK-02`)**:
   - Implement `WaitingNoticeSpec` and `emit_waiting_notice_tsx` in `crates/vox-codegen/src/web_ir/async_state.rs` enforcing non-empty `reason`, `resume_at`, and `action_href` (`data-testid="vox-waiting-notice"`).
