<objective>
Add the comparison surface that turns two or more eval-corpus reports into honest pairwise claims: exact McNemar with Holm across the preregistered family, paired cluster bootstrap for pass@k, and the resolution floor beside every comparison (BENCH-03; D-04). CONTEXT defaults pick a `vox model eval-compare` subcommand as the surface.

Purpose: `bootstrap_ci`, `paired_compare`, `holm_reject` and `min_detectable_difference` exist with zero callers, and no compare command exists, so the only comparison anyone can make today is eyeballing point estimates. The claim family is "each non-baseline arm vs the baseline", in the order the reports are given; that is the family a campaign preregisters (Phase 7 owns the signed preregistration itself). Until 12-09 adds bundle verification, inputs are typed reports and eligibility is re-derived from their provenance rather than trusted.
Output: `crates/vox-eval/src/compare.rs`, `crates/vox-cli/src/commands/model/eval_compare.rs`, subcommand registration, regenerated command catalogs.
</objective>
