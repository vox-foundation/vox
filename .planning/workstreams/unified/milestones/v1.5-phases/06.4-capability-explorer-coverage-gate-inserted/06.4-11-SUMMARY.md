     - `PageDown` / `PageUp`: moves 10 rows (clamped).

     - `ArrowLeft`: moves focus to previous action button, from first action to row, or collapses expanded parent.
     - `Escape`: on an action returns focus to the row.
     - `Enter` / `Space`: toggles expandable row or activates action button.
   - `rangeExtractor` in virtual list preserves `activeIndex` so the focused row remains mounted regardless of scroll offset.
