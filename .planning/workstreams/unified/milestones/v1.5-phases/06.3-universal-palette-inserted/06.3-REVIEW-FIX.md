### WR-10: Failed or late keybind load pins defaults and the next rebind overwrites saved bindings
**Files modified:** `lib/keybindStore.ts`, `lib/keybindStore.test.ts`
**Commit:** 2ea6590bc
**Applied fix:** the store tracks `ready` (initial load succeeded) separately from `loading`; a failed load resets so the next subscribe or rebind retries; rebinds made before `ready` are held as a delta and merged over the saved bindings once they load, and nothing is persisted before then. Existing tests that expected identity (`toBe`) or immediate persistence were updated to the new semantics. Not done: persist failure is still swallowed (memory keeps the value); surfacing it in Settings was not in the brief. Edge: a "reset to defaults" issued before the first successful load produces an empty delta and is not persisted.
**Status:** fixed: requires human verification (state-handling logic)
