5. **Phase 42 (`BENCH-07..13`)**: `should_dispatch_benchmark_campaign` and `assemble_triggered_benchmark_report` enforcing `E1..E6` estimands, conflict-of-interest disclosure, and `E5` suppression on external anchor drift.
