   - Asserts false-positive rate is under 5%.
3. Implement `crates/vox-doc-pipeline/src/claims/findings.rs`:
   - Converts dead/missing mentions with `MentionRole::Prescribes` into Reality Audit findings.
   - Computes deterministic SHA-256 fingerprint: `sha256(format!("{}:{}:{}:{}", doc_path, raw_text, kind, line))`.
   - Deduplicates findings by fingerprint.
   - Calculates priority score using the formula `Impact*2 + BlastRadius*2 + Staleness + EnforcementGap + Tractability`.
   - Generates updated `metrics.v1.json` distinguishing `unpopulated` (0 scanned) vs `healthy` (scanned > 0, open = 0) vs `deficits_found`.
</action>
