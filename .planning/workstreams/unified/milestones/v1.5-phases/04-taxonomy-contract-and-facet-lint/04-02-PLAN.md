## Decisions applied

- **D-07 (reversible):** about 10 coarse domains and 40-60 topics, each topic `broader` a domain, hand-seeded from where-things-live.md with obvious synonyms and crate names as altLabels. `topic`/`domain` are new axes, independent of `category`/`status`.
- **Discretion, label identity:** the prefLabel is itself the value written in frontmatter (`topic: orchestration`), lowercase kebab-case; there is no separate id field. Retiring a label means moving it into the replacement concept's `deprecatedAliases`.
- **Discretion, altLabels in search:** query-time expansion to a quoted facet phrase that matches only facet-tagged card chunks. Bare synonym terms are NOT added to the query: they would move Phase 1 retrieval metrics, and any such change lands only with a recorded eval delta (EMB-06 rule). Because no doc in the frozen eval snapshot carries a facet line, this expansion leaves `vox kb eval` byte-identical.
