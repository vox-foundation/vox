# Plan 453-01: Reasoning-Budget Separation (`LIVE-09`) & Citation/Verifier/Cache Accuracy (`LIVE-10`)

## Objective
Prevent reasoning-token exhaustion from truncating or zeroing out research syntheses, scope `audit_citations` precision strictly to inline `[N]` citations referenced in the report, eliminate contrastive `"not only"` false-contradictions in `try_fast_lexical_verify`, and harden `research_cache` keys and abstention filtering.

## Tasks

1. **Decouple Reasoning vs. Visible Output Budgets (`LIVE-09`):**
   - Add `pub reasoning_max_tokens: Option<u64>` to `ChatParams` (`crates/vox-llm-egress/src/lib.rs`) and `LlmConfig` (`crates/vox-actor-runtime/src/llm/types.rs`, threaded in `chat.rs`).
   - On OpenRouter wire requests in `crates/vox-llm-egress/src/wire.rs`, serialize `reasoning: { "max_tokens": N }` when `reasoning_max_tokens` is `Some(N)` (`skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none"` so all existing callers stay byte-identical).
   - Update `ResearchConfig::default()` (`config.rs`): `synthesis_max_tokens = 12_000`, `judge_max_tokens = 6_000`, and cap synthesis reasoning tokens (`reasoning_max_tokens = Some(4_000)`) in `stages.rs`.
   - In `cascade.rs` (`chat_with_cascade`), iterate candidates when an earlier candidate returns `content.trim().is_empty()` with `finish_reason == Some("length")` or `reasoning_tokens > 0` so thinking exhaustion falls back cleanly to the next candidate.

2. **Citation-Scoped Precision, `"not only"` Guard & Cache Key Hygiene (`LIVE-10`):**
   - In `pipeline.rs`, parse 1-based `[N]` citation indices from `answer` (`extract_referenced_citation_indices`), scope `audit_citations` precision to the referenced citations when non-empty, and include high-ranked/diverse hits up to `16` during pre-synthesis claim extraction so claims beyond `all_hits[..8]` are verified.
   - In `verifier.rs`, strip contrastive idioms (`"not only"`, `"not just"`, `"not merely"`) before `has_negation_token` evaluation and add unit tests proving `"not only improves X, but also Y"` does not conflict with positive evidence.
   - In `pipeline_cache.rs`, include `query.domain_mode`, `query.waves`, and `query.site_scope` in `research_cache_key`, and skip storing/serving empty answers or answers starting with `ABSTENTION_MARKER` (`[INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE]`).
