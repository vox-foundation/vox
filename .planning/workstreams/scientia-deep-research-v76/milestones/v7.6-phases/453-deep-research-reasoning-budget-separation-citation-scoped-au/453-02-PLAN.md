# Plan 453-02: Markdown-Aware Claim Decomposer, Novelty Overlap Projection, Empirical Science Class & Daemon DB Proxy (`LIVE-11..12`)

## Objective
Clean Markdown/YAML/HTML artifacts before claim extraction while preserving byte-span integrity, project prior-art overlap into `scientia_evidence`, classify empirical domain science accurately, include research runs in quarterly Scientia cost rollups, and transparently proxy CLI `vox research` / `vox scientia` commands through daemon IPC when canonical `vox.db` holds a Turso `flock`.

## Tasks

1. **Markdown/Frontmatter Sanitization, Novelty Projection, Empirical Class & Cost Rollup (`LIVE-11`):**
   - Mask YAML frontmatter (`--- ... ---` at byte 0), HTML comments (`<!-- ... -->`), and Markdown table separator/pipe rows with spaces of identical byte length in `vox_scientia::claim_extractor::pipeline` (and trim leading/trailing Markdown heading/pipe tokens in `AtomicDecomposer::decompose` with exact byte-offset adjustment) so `SpanChecker::check` continues to match `source_text[start..end]` verbatim while zero frontmatter/HTML/table fragments become claims.
   - In `crates/vox-cli-research/src/db/publication/discovery.rs` (`merge_novelty_bundle_into_metadata_json_str`), compute `max_lexical_score` from `bundle.normalized_hits` (and `prior_art_query_succeeded` from `bundle.query_traces`) and write `prior_art_max_lexical_overlap`, `max_lexical_score`, and `prior_art_query_succeeded` into `root["scientia_evidence"]` so `publication-discovery-scan` drops `novelty_overlap_assumed_default`.
   - Add `EmpiricalScience` to `vox_research_events::FindingCandidateClass` and `vox_scientia::class_routing::FindingClass`, and detect empirical science terms (electrolyte, ionic conductivity, crystal, synthesis, catalyst, protein, genome, thermodynamics, spectroscopy, etc.) in `discovery_bridge::infer_candidate_class`.
   - Add `#[serde(default)] pub research_usd: f64` to `QuarterlyCostSummary` and `CostInputs` (`vox_scientia::dashboard::cost`) and accumulate `"research" | "general" | "codegen" | "shopping"` phases in `review_flow::assemble_scientia_cost_from_db` and `scientia_phase_handlers::apply_phase_costs` so `this_quarter.total_usd` matches `by_provider`.

2. **Daemon IPC Auto-Proxy on `vox.db` Turso `flock` Contention (`LIVE-12`):**
   - Create `<400`-LoC `crates/vox-cli-research/src/daemon_db_proxy.rs` with `is_db_lock_error(&anyhow::Error) -> bool` (detecting `"Failed locking file"`, `"DatabaseBusy"`, `"locked"`, `"Resource temporarily unavailable"`) when `VOX_DB_PATH` is not explicitly set to a custom standalone file.
   - Wire `vox research {run,history,show,result,watch,search}` (`lib.rs`) and `vox scientia {dashboard,cost,retrieval-status,claims,publication-status,...}` (`scientia.rs` / `scientia_phase_handlers.rs`) to automatically route through `daemon_tool::daemon_client()` (`API_V2_GET`, `TOOL_CALL`, and synchronous wait on `dei_method::RESEARCH_RUN`) whenever canonical `vox.db` is locked by a live daemon/GUI process.
