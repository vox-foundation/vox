## Rubric applied

- `none`: only computes and prints, or reads purely in-process state.
- `read_only`: reads files, DB, network (GET), git; writes nothing specific to the verb under ANY flag. Transient tempdir scratch that is deleted before return (`bundle verify`, `doc-inventory verify`) and bootstrap of the primary workspace DB on open do not count (ambient).
- `writes_files`: some flag or the default path writes files, caches, DB rows, or creates a verb-specific store.
- Budget per verb: at most two scoped searches plus one read of 60 lines or fewer. One verb ran out (memory search) and stays null.
