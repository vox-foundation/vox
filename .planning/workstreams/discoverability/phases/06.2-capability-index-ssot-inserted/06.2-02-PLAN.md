<objective>
Fill the Location and grouping fields of every operation row, and add the D-05 ratchet:

- the owning crate and its rule (D-04)
- the layer from layers.toml
- the source path
- the curated domain from a committed vocabulary (D-09)
- the catalog tier

With the ratchet, `vox ci capability-index` fails if the number of rows with no resolvable owner rises.

Purpose: DISC-01 needs a crate and layer on every row, and `vox features --domain <d>` needs a validated domain. DISC-02 needs a gate that stops the index from silently losing ownership as commands are added.

Output: owner.rs, ratchet.rs, domains.v1.yaml, owner-ratchet.v1.json, optional `owner_crate` / `domain` catalog fields, `vox ci capability-index --tighten`.
</objective>
