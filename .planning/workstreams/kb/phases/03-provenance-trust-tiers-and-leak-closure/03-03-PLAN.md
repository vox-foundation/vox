<assumption_delta_decision>
primary_noun: knowledge-base entry
before: a `kb_entries` row inside a user-named `knowledge_bases` bucket; `accepted=1` by default; immediately retrievable through `kb_lines`, @-mentionable by bucket name, and exported as MENS SFT data
after: two nouns. A curated Library document (merged git, indexed by Phase 2, Trusted or SystemVerified) and a finding (`kb_findings` inbox row, Untrusted or Advisory, status pending, never auto-injected). A finding never upgrades in place; it becomes knowledge only through a reviewed PR in Phase 9 that adds a page alongside human text
decision: promote
rationale: the finding inbox plus the git-canonical index replaces the mutable DB store as the primary model (practices SSOT section 5, RET-02). Keeping `kb_entries` alongside would leave a second unreviewed retrieval path, the R1/R2 leak class this phase closes. `status`, `trust_tier` and "injectable" stay three separate concepts; no `kb_findings` status is ever treated as curated by retrieval
invariant_test: `crates/vox-db/tests/tiered_writer_invariant.rs::search_corpus_and_inbox_have_only_tiered_writers` (created in 03-05: no write path reaches `search_documents` or `kb_findings` except through the tier-requiring writers), backed by the `compile_fail` doctests on `NewKbFinding` (this plan) and `upsert_search_document` (03-05)
</assumption_delta_decision>
