## Assumptions pending discussion

- **MENS from findings (Open Question 4):** DPO pairs only, from rows with `reviewed_by` set and status approved/rejected, paired by provenance query; no SFT. Phase 3 produces no reviewed rows, so the stage emits nothing until Phase 9. Alternative: SFT on approved findings (conflicts with Pitfall 3 and TAX-06).
- **Legacy `accepted=1` entries are downgraded to pending Untrusted findings** (the old acceptance is preserved in provenance only).
- **Migration runs at every `ServerState` DB attach (A7);** idempotent and near-free at 0 rows. Alternative: an explicit `vox kb migrate-legacy` command.
- **Already-trained MENS data is not reversible:** rows with `mens_queued=1` were already exported as SFT; recorded in the CHANGELOG by 03-13.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| legacy kb_entries (auto-accepted, partly model output) → kb_findings | migrated text must not gain trust |
| kb_findings → MENS training mix | unreviewed text must not become training data |

|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-13 | Tampering | `run_kb_signals_stage` | high | mitigate | Reads only reviewed rows (SQL filter plus pure-function re-check), DPO only, no SFT; mutation-proven |
| T-03-14 | Elevation of privilege | `migrate_legacy_kb_entries` | medium | mitigate | Every migrated row is pending and Untrusted regardless of the legacy `accepted` flag; content sanitized and redacted by `capture_finding` |
| T-03-29 | Denial of service | migration at startup | low | mitigate | Spawned, paged, idempotent; errors logged, never fatal |
