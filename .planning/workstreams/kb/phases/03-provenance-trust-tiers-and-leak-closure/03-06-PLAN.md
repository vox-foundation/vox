<objective>
Route every research path (quick turn, deep turn, deep-research gathering, GUI "Save to KB") into the `kb_findings` inbox and remove the research path that writes LLM answers into `docs/src`.

Purpose: TRUST-02 (sinks S3, S4, S15-via-gather), TRUST-03 (the deep synthesis is model output and must not re-enter the KB at elevated trust), TRUST-04, and the research part of GROW-06. A latent sink found during planning is closed too: `pipeline.rs` writes the synthesis into `docs/src/research/` through `write_research_doc` when `persist_to_docs` is set. Every caller passes false today, but Phase 2 indexes `docs/src/**` as Trusted, so the branch is an unreviewed path into curated docs.
Output: `capture_research_sources` and `capture_research_synthesis` in research_turn.rs, `ResearchConfig.finding_capture`, gather capture in web_gather.rs, the docs-writing branch deleted.
</objective>

## Assumptions pending discussion

- **Deep synthesis is Untrusted (Open Question 1 / A5):** an LLM synthesis of web input inherits Untrusted by the taint rule. Alternative: Advisory per the literal "agent-derived" wording of TRUST-01.
- **Deep turn captures only the synthesis finding;** its sources are captured once by the gather step, and dedupe absorbs any overlap.
- **The latent `persist_to_docs` docs writer is deleted,** not rerouted. The `persist_to_docs` field stays, deprecation-marked, because many constructors set it. Alternative: reroute it to a `publish_research_doc`-style finding.
- **The `research_cache` knowledge node (S14) keeps being written** as a cache; 03-09 excludes it from search. Alternative: move the cache out of `knowledge_nodes`.
