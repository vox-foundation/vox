<objective>
Route every research path (quick turn, deep turn, deep-research gathering, GUI "Save to KB") into the `kb_findings` inbox, and remove every research path that writes LLM output into `docs/src`.

Purpose: TRUST-02 (sinks S3, S4), TRUST-03 (the deep synthesis is model output and must not re-enter the KB at elevated trust), TRUST-04, and the research part of GROW-06. Two latent `docs/src` writers are closed too: the shim pipeline's `persist_to_docs` branch (every caller passes false today, but Phase 2 indexes `docs/src/**` as Trusted) and the CLI `vox research publish` command, which is the defactored twin of the GUI `publish_research_doc` (`crates/vox-cli-research/src/lib.rs:438`) and writes `docs/src/architecture/<slug>-2026.md` directly.
Output: `capture_research_sources` and `capture_research_synthesis`, `ResearchConfig.finding_capture`, gather capture in `web_gather.rs`, the docs-writing branch and writer deleted, `vox research publish` redirected to a local draft.
</objective>

## Decisions applied

- **D-06 taint rule:** the deep synthesis is Untrusted, not Advisory. Every research finding is Untrusted.
- **D-05:** CLI research paths have no capture sink (no edge to the mcp capture helper), so `vox research publish` writes a local draft and files no finding; GUI paths file findings. Accepted interim asymmetry.
- Deep turns capture only the synthesis finding; their sources are captured once by the gather step, and dedupe absorbs overlap.
- The `research_cache` knowledge node (sink S14) keeps being written as a cache; 03-09 excludes it from search.
