<objective>
Make retrieval honor the stored tiers: label hits, keep Untrusted out of the automatic chat preamble, derive the envelope tier from the hits, and sanitize at the injection boundary. The SSOT and where-things-live updates for the whole phase are in 03-13.

Purpose: TRUST-01 (every row maps onto the tier vocabulary when it is served, and the `retrieval.rs:148` blanket Trusted stamp goes), TRUST-05 at the injection boundary (rows that predate the sanitizer, and curated docs, are sanitized again on the way to the model), RET-02 (one vox-search retrieval path, now tier-aware). Closes research Pitfall 6: legacy web rows would otherwise keep flowing into chat.
Output: tier lookups in vox-db, `SearchPolicy::exclude_untrusted_hits`, tier-labelled lines and `SearchExecution::min_trust_tier`, sanitized bundle/sources/KB-tool output.
</objective>

## Assumptions pending discussion

- **Dropping Untrusted from the automatic preamble now (A6)**, before Phase 7 spotlighting: chat gets less autonomous web context until then; explicit search tools still return Untrusted hits, labelled. Alternative: keep them in the preamble behind a label until Phase 7.
- **`research_cache` nodes are excluded from search entirely** (they are a cache, not knowledge), so a deep turn adds no searchable row. Alternative: move the cache out of `knowledge_nodes`.
- **Memory-file lines are Advisory and repository lines Trusted** for the envelope minimum.
