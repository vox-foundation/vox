class="vd-diagram" style="font-family:var(--vd-font,system-ui,sans-serif);font-size:14px">`, then
`<title id="P-title">`, `<desc id="P-desc">`, `<defs>` with one `<marker id="P-arrow" viewBox="0 0 10 10"
refX="9" refY="5" markerWidth="8" markerHeight="8" orient="auto">` holding a triangle `<path>`, then groups
in draw order `vd-containers` (85-03), `vd-edges`, `vd-nodes`, `vd-edge-labels` (85-02). A node is
`<g class="vd-node">` with exactly one shape element of class `vd-node-shape` (a `rect`, or a `polygon` for
`diamond`) followed by an optional `<text class="vd-label" text-anchor="middle">`; nodes appear in IR order.
An edge is `<path class="vd-edge" d=".." marker-end="url(#P-arrow)">`, in IR order. Paint declarations may sit
on the wrapping `<g>` (inherited) or on the element, always inside `style`.
