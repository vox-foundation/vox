Created here:
- `limits::MAX_RENDER_ACTORS: usize = 100` and `limits::MAX_RENDER_EVENTS: usize = 200`, `///` docs saying they
  are the sequence render caps, kept beside the graph render caps.
- `layout::seq::MSG_WRAP_CELLS = 60`: message and note text lines come from `text::wrap_at(text, 60)`; actor
  labels keep `text::wrap` (28 cells, the graph node rule).
- Self-message gap rule: when lane i has a right neighbour, gap_i is also at least `SELF_W + SELF_LABEL_GAP (6
  px) + label box width + LANE_CLEAR` for every labelled self-message on lane i.
- Validation (`layout::seq`, before any placement), in this order: `actors.len() > MAX_RENDER_ACTORS ||
  events.len() > MAX_RENDER_EVENTS` gives `TooLarge { nodes: actors, edges: events }`; a repeated actor id gives
  `InvalidIr("actor {i}: duplicate id")`; an actor label, message text or note text over `MAX_LABEL_CHARS`
  gives `InvalidIr` through `check_label("actor {i}" | "event {i}", ..)`; a note whose `over` is empty or longer
  than two gives `InvalidIr("event {i}: a note covers one or two actors")`; an unknown actor keeps 86-01's
  `InvalidIr("event {i}: unknown actor")`. `RenderError::TooLarge` field docs say "nodes plus containers
  (graph) or actors (sequence)" and "edges (graph) or events (sequence)".
- `tests/support/svgcheck/seq.rs` (declared `pub mod seq;` in `tests/support/svgcheck.rs`):
  `pub fn assert_sequence_geometry(svg: &Svg, ir: &SequenceIr) -> Result<(), String>` and `pub fn
  sequence_ir() -> impl Strategy<Value = SequenceIr>` (1-6 actors with labels up to 40 characters; 0-14
  events: messages between any two actors including self, text 0-130 characters drawn from short words, long
  sentences, CJK, emoji and explicit `\n`, `dashed` random; notes over one or two actors with non-blank text up
  to 130 characters; every text at most `MAX_LABEL_CHARS`). The oracle reads only the checker's output plus the
  IR's order (actors to `nodes`/`lifelines`, messages to `edge_paths`, messages with non-blank text to
  `edge_labels`, notes to `notes`) and asserts: every box inside `[0, width] x [0, height]`; actor boxes
  pairwise disjoint; note boxes pairwise disjoint; every message label box disjoint from every other label box,
  actor box and note box; each lifeline's x is its actor box centre and its top is the box bottom; a
  non-self message has two points on its endpoint lifelines at one y and its label box lies strictly between
  those lifelines with at least 8 px on each side; a self-message has four points starting and ending on its
  lifeline, its label box lies right of the loop and, when a next lane exists, at least 8 px before that
  lifeline; a one-lane note is centred on its lifeline (within one half-pixel) and a two-lane note's box
  spans both lifelines. It never imports anything from `src` beyond the public IR types.
