<objective>
Add a sampling seed to the model-agnostic LLM facade and its OpenAI-compatible wire, so a benchmark run can pass and record the seed it requested (BENCH-02, D-12, D-03 "the seed is recorded and passed").

Purpose: the facade has no seed anywhere today (`LlmConfig`, `ChatParams` and `OpenAiChatRequest` carry temperature/top_p/max_tokens only). Every later plan that claims a seed (12-02 eval-corpus, 12-03 MENS serve) depends on this field existing end to end. `top_p` already exists at every layer and is the shape to copy.
Output: `seed` on `ChatParams`, `OpenAiChatRequest`, `LlmConfig`; forwarding at the three facade construction sites; hermetic wiremock tests; one mutation proof.
</objective>
