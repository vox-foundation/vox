<objective>
Close the two in-band lowering paths for oversight on the task-completion and task-submit sides (FLOOR-03, roadmap SC-3).

Purpose: `effective_risk_for_task` returns `task.risk_posture.or(category).or(source)`, so a self-assigned `RiskPosture::High` hint turns grounding and Socrates off and loosens the approval tier (research Pattern 4). Separately, `trust_relax_gates` silently skips both requeue arms, including the `bypass_disallowed` arm (Pitfall 2). This plan makes strictest-wins the rule and narrows trust relax to hint-added enforcement only.

Output: new strictest-wins helpers in `mode.rs`, a reshaped resolver plus gate conditions in `socrates.rs`, the tier input change in `attention_fields.rs`, and inverted plus new call-site tests.
</objective>
