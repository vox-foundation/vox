Execute the 3-Way Separation of Concerns (SoC) split for `vox-gamify` and Ludus (`LUDUS-SOC-01..05`):
1. **Tier 1 (`LUDUS-SOC-01`, `LUDUS-SOC-02`):** Move `crates/vox-gamify/src/db/agent_telemetry.rs` into `crates/vox-db/src/store/ops_agent_telemetry.rs`, rewire all callers in `vox-orchestrator` and `vox-orchestrator-mcp`, and delete unconsented shadow telemetry (`crates/vox-gamify/src/db/hint_telemetry.rs`, `crates/vox-gamify/src/lsp_telemetry.rs`).
2. **Tier 2 (`LUDUS-SOC-03`, `LUDUS-SOC-04`):** Move multi-user competitive/social Ludus (`collegium`, `arena`, `disputes`, `leaderboards`, and CLI `ludus/{collegium,arena,disputes,auth,sync}.rs`) into `~/dev/vox-ops/crates/vox-ops-ludus/` backed by PostgreSQL `ludus_community.*`.
3. **Tier 3 (`LUDUS-SOC-05`):** Verify solo developer progression (`LudusProfile`, `Companion`, `Quest`, `Battle`, `StreakTracker`, `AchievementTracker`, `shop`, `sprite_svg`, and FSRS discovery `discovery/{fsrs,rank,ledger,scientia_kudos}.rs`) works 100% offline in `vox-gamify`.

Purpose: Eliminate architectural coupling where `vox-orchestrator` depended on `vox-gamify` for tenant token budgets and LLM cost records, eliminate unconsented shadow telemetry in `vox-lsp`/`vox-gamify`, and separate multi-user competitive state from solo local progression.
Output: `crates/vox-db/src/store/ops_agent_telemetry.rs`, cleaned `crates/vox-gamify`, and `~/dev/vox-ops/crates/vox-ops-ludus`.
