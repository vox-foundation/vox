# Phase 290 Plan 01: High-Detail Terrace Grounds: Orthogonal `Cardo`/`Decumanus` Streets, `Opus Sectile` Marble Plazas, Vaulted `Substructio` Cliff Arcades & Balustrades

**Phase:** 290 (`290-high-detail-terrace-grounds-and-substructio`)
**Requirements:** `URBS-GROUNDS-01`, `URBS-GROUNDS-02`, `URBS-GROUNDS-03`, `URBS-GROUNDS-04`

## Goal
Transform flat district terraces and plain cliff retaining walls into richly articulated Roman urban grounds featuring recessed basalt `Cardo`/`Decumanus` streets with raised travertine curbstones and stepping-stone crosswalks, central `Opus Sectile` geometric marble forum plazas around District Wonders, vaulted `Substructio` blind arcades and buttress pilasters on cliff walls, and perimeter stone balustrades with boundary obelisks (`Termini`).

## Files
- `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/components/gamify/urbs/worldRenderer.ts`

## Tasks
1. **Orthogonal `Cardo` & `Decumanus` Basalt Streets (`URBS-GROUNDS-01`)**:
   - Implement `drawDistrictGroundDetails` in `worldRenderer.ts` rendering recessed basalt thoroughfares along the primary axes of each district terrace with raised travertine curbstones (`Crepido`) and stepping-stone crosswalks.
2. **Inlaid `Opus Sectile` Marble Forum Plazas (`URBS-GROUNDS-02`)**:
   - Render concentric geometric marble forum inlays (travertine, porphyry, and serpentine borders + central compass medallion) beneath each District Wonder.
3. **Vaulted `Substructio` Cliff Arcades & Buttress Pilasters (`URBS-GROUNDS-03`)**:
   - Upgrade `paintFullBleedRomanLandscape` in `worldRenderer.ts` to articulate the SW shadowed and SE sunlit cliff retaining walls with recessed arched vaults, projecting stone pilasters, and drainage weep-spouts.
4. **Terrace Parapet Balustrades & Boundary Obelisks (`URBS-GROUNDS-04`)**:
   - Add carved stone parapet coping, corner Roman boundary obelisks (`Termini`), and manicured cypress/olive planter beds (`Horti`).
