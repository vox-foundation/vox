# Plan 284-01: Sequenced `ResumableTurnStreamRegistry`, Full `ThreadRunQueue` Lifecycle, Real `ModelSpec` Cost Ledger & `SessionPrivacyPosture` Gate

## Goal
Close Cluster 2 of `docs/src/architecture/t3-vox-wave5-deep-review-and-parity-closure-2026.md`:
1. Replace `ServerState::resumable_streams` tuple map with `ResumableTurnStreamRegistry` (`SequencedTurnChunk`, multi-iteration accumulation across tool loops and direct streaming turns, `vox_chat_stream_resume` + `vox_chat_stream_cancel`).
2. Wire `ThreadRunQueue` (`pop_next_follow_up`, `register_background_work`/`complete_background_work`/`can_settle_turn`, and 429 `LimitRecoveryState`).
3. Move `check_preflight_balance` after model resolution in `message.rs`, wire `ModelRegistry::compute_turn_cost_delta` into `persist_and_envelope.rs`, add rolling 4-hour burst + monthly quota windows to `BatteryAndCostLedger`, and expose `vox_battery_status`.
4. Gate SQLite/disk persistence on `SessionPrivacyPosture::allows_disk_persistence()` in `persist_and_envelope.rs`.
