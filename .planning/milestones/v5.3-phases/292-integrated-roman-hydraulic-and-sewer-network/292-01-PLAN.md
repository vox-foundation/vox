# Phase 292 Plan 01: Integrated Roman Hydraulic Plumbing & Sewer Network (`hydraulicNetwork.ts`): `Aquaeductus` Trunk, `Castellum Divisorium`, `Fistulae` Supply Pipes & `Cloaca Maxima` Sewer Trenches/Outfalls

**Phase:** 292 (`292-integrated-roman-hydraulic-and-sewer-network`)
**Requirements:** `URBS-HYDRAULIC-01`, `URBS-HYDRAULIC-02`, `URBS-HYDRAULIC-03`, `URBS-HYDRAULIC-04`

## Goal
Create `hydraulicNetwork.ts` integrating the `AQVAE MCP` aqueduct directly into an upper-terrace `Castellum Divisorium` header tank that feeds a SimCity-style Roman hydraulic plumbing (`Fistulae` twin-rail bronze pipes & sluice valves) and `Cloaca Maxima` arched stone sewer trench network across all district terraces, terminating in cliff-base `Cloaca` sea outfalls and stepped cascades (`Cataracta`).

## Tasks
1. **Integrated `Aquaeductus` Trunk & `Castellum Divisorium` (`URBS-HYDRAULIC-01`)**:
   - Create `hydraulicNetwork.ts` implementing `drawRomanHydraulicInfrastructure` that bridges the `AQVAE MCP` aqueduct into an upper-terrace `Castellum Divisorium` (Main Hydraulic Distribution Chamber) and routes elevated `Specus` stone channels into the district network.
2. **Pressurized `Fistulae` Supply Conduits & Bronze Sluice Valves (`Epistomia`) (`URBS-HYDRAULIC-02`)**:
   - Route isometric twin-rail bronze/lead `Fistulae` pipes along terrace street curbs from district hydraulic headers to active file worksites and inter-district viaducts, with bronze junction manifolds (`Calices`) and rotary valve wheels.
3. **Recessed `Cloaca Maxima` Sewer Trenches & Inspection Grates (`Spiracula`) (`URBS-HYDRAULIC-03`)**:
   - Render stone-lined `Cloaca` drainage trenches and circular bronze manhole grates along the `Cardo`/`Decumanus` street grid.
4. **Cliff-Wall `Cloaca` Sea Outfalls & Stepped Sluice Cascades (`Cataracta`) (`URBS-HYDRAULIC-04`)**:
   - Render arched stone `Cloaca Maxima` sewer outfall portals at the base of coastal cliffs discharging water/error plumes into the sea.
