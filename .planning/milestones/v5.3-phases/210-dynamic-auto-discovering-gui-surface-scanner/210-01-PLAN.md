# Phase 210 Plan 01: Dynamic Auto-Discovering GUI Surface, Sub-Tab, Dock & Popover CV Scanner

**Phase:** 210
**Plan:** 210-01
**Requirements:** `CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-01`, `CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-02`, `CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-03`, `CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-04`

## Tasks

1. **Create `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/lib/cvSurfaceDiscovery.ts` & `cvSurfaceDiscovery.test.ts` (`CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-01`)**
   - Dynamically introspect `SURFACE_REGISTRY`, `surfaceDecorators`, `PARENT_CHILD_MAP`, `TOP_LEVEL_VIEWS`, `LEGACY_VIEW_ALIASES`, and `CHAT_DOCK_PANEL_IDS` to produce a single `GuiSurfaceDiscoveryManifest` with zero hardcoded view arrays.
   - Update `crates/vox-gui/ui/e2e/review/states.ts` to derive its base `SURFACE_STATES` map dynamically from `SURFACE_REGISTRY` instead of a hardcoded 32-element array.

2. **Add In-Page Sub-Tab & Popover Discovery + Clipping/Row-Wrap/Duplication Metrics (`CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-02`, `CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-03`)**
   - Update `crates/vox-gui/ui/e2e/review/audits.ts` and `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/lib/cvFlywheel.ts` with `composerToolbarRowCount`, `composerToolbarHeightPx`, `duplicateControlGroupCount`, `discoveredSubTabLabels`, and `discoveredPopoverTriggers`, plus right-edge container clipping detection on buttons inside rails/cards.

3. **Upgrade `crates/vox-gui/ui/e2e/review/cv-flywheel.spec.ts` (`CV-AUTO-DISCOVER-04`)**
   - Use `discoverAllGuiSurfacesAndPanels()` to drive the all-surface CV scan, dynamically probe sub-tabs on multi-tab surfaces, and emit `discoveryManifest` + per-surface sub-tab/popover/layout telemetry in `cv-flywheel-all-surfaces.json`.
   - Verify with Vitest (`cvSurfaceDiscovery.test.ts` + `reviewStates.guard.test.ts` + `cvFlywheel.test.ts`) and Playwright.
