# Phase 291 Plan 01: High-Detail Vitruvian Building Architecture (`T0`–`T3`): Rusticated Ground Arcades (`Fornices`), Belt Cornices, Atrium `Impluvia` & `Acroteria` Roofs

**Phase:** 291 (`291-high-detail-vitruvian-building-architecture`)
**Requirements:** `URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-01`, `URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-02`, `URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-03`, `URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-04`

## Goal
Enrich all `T0`–`T3` file buildings in `worldRenderer.ts` with ground-floor rusticated stone arcades (`Fornices`) and warm shopfront lamplight, projecting inter-story `Marcapiano` belt cornices and corner quoins, recessed atrium `Impluvium` rainwater basins in `T1` Domus courtyards, and sculpted rooftop `Acroteria` finials and brick hypocaust chimneys.

## Files
- `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/components/gamify/urbs/worldRenderer.ts`

## Tasks
1. **Ground-Floor Rusticated Stone Arcades (`Fornices`) & Keystones (`URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-01`)**:
   - Extend `drawIsoBox` in `worldRenderer.ts` with `groundArchesSW` and `groundArchesSE` options that render recessed arched shopfront/loggia openings with stone keystone voussoirs and warm interior lantern glow on `T0` Tabernae and `T2` Insulae.
2. **Inter-Story Stringcourse Belt Cornices (`Marcapiano`) & Quoins (`URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-02`)**:
   - Add `beltCourses` and corner ashlar quoining to `drawIsoBox` for `T1`, `T2`, and `T3` buildings.
3. **Recessed Atrium `Impluvium` Rainwater Basins (`URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-03`)**:
   - Upgrade `T1` Domus in `drawIsoFileBuilding` with a sunken marble `Impluvium` courtyard pool reflecting sky-cyan water and a columned prostyle porch.
4. **Sculpted Rooftop `Acroteria` Finials & Hypocaust Chimneys (`URBS-ARCH-DETAIL-04`)**:
   - Upgrade `drawIsoPitchedRoof` with golden-ochre gable `Acroteria` ornaments, stepped pediment mouldings, and terracotta brick chimney flues.
