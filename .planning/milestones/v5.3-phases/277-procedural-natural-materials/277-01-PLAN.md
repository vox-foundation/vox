# Phase 277 Plan 01: Procedural Natural Surface Material Synthesis (`pbrMaterials.ts`)

## Objective
Replace flat solid-color hex fills with a deterministic offscreen procedural material synthesis engine (`pbrMaterials.ts`) producing cached `CanvasPattern`s and physically-inspired metallic/surface gradients for Pitted Roman Travertine Ashlar, Scalloped Terracotta Roof Tiles, Opus Incertum Flagstone Plazas, Stratified Basalt Cliffs, Weathered Oak Timber, and Anisotropic Hammered Bronze.

## Tasks
1. **Task 1**: Create `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/components/gamify/urbs/pbrMaterials.ts` with deterministic value-noise and micro-surface generators (`buildPbrMaterialAtlas`, `createVerticalWallGradient`, `createBronzeMetallicGradient`, `createIronMetallicGradient`).
2. **Task 2**: Create `crates/vox-gui/ui/src/components/gamify/urbs/__tests__/pbrMaterials.test.ts` verifying deterministic noise, pattern generation, and shader gradient stops.
