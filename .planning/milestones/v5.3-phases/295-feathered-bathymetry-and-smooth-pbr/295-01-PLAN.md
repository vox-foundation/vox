# Phase 295 Plan 01: Feathered Coastal Bathymetry, Smooth Macro-Shaded PBR Materials & Laplacian Noise Suppression

## Objective
Eliminate hard diamond coastal cutouts, 1px Laplacian PBR pixel chatter (`noiseFloor: 10.7`), and `#1a1613` cartoon ink outlines by introducing feathered elliptical radial coastal shoals, smooth bilinear/radial macro-shaded PBR stone/terracotta/water tiles, full-canvas diagonal ocean swells, and soft tonal masonry/roof edge bevels.

## Tasks
1. Upgrade `buildTravertineTile`, `buildTerracottaTile`, `buildPlazaCobbleTile`, `buildCliffTile`, and `buildSeaWaterTile` in `pbrMaterials.ts` to use smooth gradient-shaded ashlar blocks and low-Laplacian transitions.
2. Replace hard 4-vertex polygon coastal shelves in `paintFullBleedRomanLandscape` (`worldRenderer.ts`) with feathered elliptical radial coastal shoal and surf gradients plus diagonal ocean swell ribbons.
3. Replace dark `#1a1613` cartoon outlines in `drawIsoBox` and `drawIsoPitchedRoof` (`worldRenderer.ts`) with soft tonal stone/terracotta edge bevels.
