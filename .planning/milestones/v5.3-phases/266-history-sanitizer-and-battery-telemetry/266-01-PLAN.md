# Phase 266 Plan 01: Live `context_sanitizer` History Stripping, `vox_chat_compact_up_to`, `BatteryCostLedger` Accounting, `VoxIdAllocatorV2` Message IDs & Real `ResponseQualityAnalyzer` Telemetry

## Objective

Close `GAP-03` and `GAP-04` by:
1. Wiring `sanitize_assistant_for_history` and `escape_prompt_injection_tokens` into `transcript_entry_to_llm_message` (`conversation.rs`).
2. Implementing `chat_compact_up_to` (`vox_chat_compact_up_to`) in `history.rs` and wiring it in `tool_registry.rs`, `input_schemas.rs`, and `dispatch.rs`.
3. Adding `battery_ledger: Arc<BatteryCostLedger>` and `id_allocator: VoxIdAllocatorV2` to `ServerState`, running pre-flight balance check + post-turn `record_turn_usage`, minting deterministic `usr-*` / `ast-*` message IDs via `VoxIdAllocatorV2::derive`, and recording real `ResponseQualityAnalyzer` scores in `persist_and_envelope.rs`.
