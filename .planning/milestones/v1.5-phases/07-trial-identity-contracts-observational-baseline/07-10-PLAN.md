## Flagged assumptions (spec-less probe, unclassified rows)

- **TRIAL-04 (unclassified, review manually):** recovery assumes a single runner per campaign (RESEARCH A3). A second live runner would see the first one's running rows as stale. `owner_instance_id` and `last_heartbeat_ms` are recorded so Phase 8 can add a lease. Not handled: two concurrent `--recover` invocations.
- **TELEM-01 (unclassified, review manually):** the canary covers runner-written rows and Tier A. The pipeline's own non-bridge writes into scientia tables (answers, claims) remain the shipped behavior (no policy change), and the unified LLM-turn table still stores prompt/response under the trial label. Both are Tier C/STORE-03 classification decisions for Phase 9.
- **METER-01 (unclassified, review manually):** TTFT is NotApplicable for the whole research path because `meter_response` passes `streamed=false`. The landed `llm_stream_measured` primitive is deliberately not wired here (RESEARCH Q6.6). Calls on separately spawned tasks fall outside the metering scope; none exist on the research path today.
