<objective>
Add the research pipeline's per-attempt seam that the trial runner (07-09) drives:
- a run label that becomes the LLM telemetry session (TELEM-02);
- a result-cache bypass so replicates are real runs;
- judge failure recorded as Missing (SCORE-04 R6);
- the content-bearing metrics bridge skipped in trial runs (D-12);
- a verdict-cache-hit counter for BASE-01's knowledge-reuse rate.

Non-trial research keeps today's behavior on every path.

Purpose: the trial identity must reach every LLM turn, and the baseline must not be built on cache hits or synthetic judge scores. All three are pipeline-internal, so they land here, in the crate that owns the pipeline.
Output: two `ResearchConfig` fields, four small pipeline helpers with tests, one `RunUsage` counter.
</objective>
