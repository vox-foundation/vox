# Phase 06.3 Plan 10: Shortcut sheet and shared keybind store Summary

**`?` (outside text fields) and Mod+/ open a shortcut sheet generated from `ACTION_REGISTRY` with live chords; App, Settings and the sheet now share one `useSyncExternalStore` keybind store, so a Settings rebind takes effect in the App key handler and the open sheet without a restart.**

## Accomplishments

- `lib/keybinds.ts`: `show-shortcuts` action (Mod+/), `formatChord` (⌘⇧D on mac, Ctrl+Shift+D elsewhere, dash when empty), `isEditableTarget`, `isShortcutHelpKey` (RESEARCH F-11 predicate, AltGr safe), `paletteActionRows`.
- `lib/keybindStore.ts`: single owner of `gui.keybinds`; one lazy load, synchronous notify, persist on set, failures keep in-memory state.
- `ShortcutSheet`: Radix Dialog (title, description, focus trap, Escape), rows from `ACTION_REGISTRY`, chords in `<kbd>`.
- App and SettingsView migrated off their private copies (the old bug: a Settings rebind reached the global handler only after restart).
