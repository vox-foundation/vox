## Notes for later plans

- Only `cli.plugin.remove` is destructive in the bundled index today; plan 06.3-04 classifies more. Tests that use `bundled_index()` rely only on `cli.plugin.remove` and `cli.features`.
- `confirmed` is a client-supplied flag (T-06.3-08 accepted): protects against mis-click and agent runs, not a compromised renderer.
- The TS mirror of `CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED_PREFIX` (`capabilityRun.ts`) and its parity test are plan 06.3-09's job. Callers (`transport.callTool`, Catalog form, views) may now receive `confirmation_required: <id>` and must pass `confirmed: true` after consent.

- `vox ci crate-edges` prints a stale-baseline warning for `vox-gui -> vox-db-types`; `--tighten` is always allowed but out of scope here.
