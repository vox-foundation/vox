# Phase 20.1 Plan 02 Summary: Right TOC Hide & Restore

Executed Wave 2 of Phase 20.1: Collapsible Reader Layout.

## Accomplishments
1. **TableOfContents Override (`docs-astro/src/components/TableOfContents.astro`)**:
   - Injected `#vox-toc-hide-btn` with close icon and uppercase "Hide" label.
   - Updates `document.documentElement.dataset.tocState = 'hidden'` and `localStorage.setItem('vox-toc-state', 'hidden')`.
2. **PageSidebar Override (`docs-astro/src/components/PageSidebar.astro`)**:
   - Injected `#vox-toc-restore-btn` edge button.
   - Updates `document.documentElement.dataset.tocState = 'visible'` and `localStorage.setItem('vox-toc-state', 'visible')`.
3. **Reader Layout Stylesheet (`docs-astro/src/styles/reader-layout.css`)**:
   - Desktop rules (`@media (min-width: 72rem)`) hiding right sidebar and displaying floating pill restore button.
   - Smooth hover, focus rings, and reduced motion compliance.
