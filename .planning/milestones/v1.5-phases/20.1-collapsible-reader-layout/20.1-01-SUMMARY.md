# Phase 20.1 Plan 01 Summary: Left Rail & Hover Expansion

Executed Wave 1 of Phase 20.1: Collapsible Reader Layout.

## Accomplishments
1. **Early Head Script (`docs-astro/astro.config.mjs`)**:
   - Injected synchronous initialization in `<head>` reading `'vox-sidebar-state'` and `'vox-toc-state'` from `localStorage`.
   - Guaranteed 0ms FOUC on initial paint.
2. **Sidebar Override (`docs-astro/src/components/Sidebar.astro`)**:
   - Injected `#vox-sidebar-pin-toggle` above sidebar content with dock icon.
   - Synchronized toggle state with `document.documentElement.dataset.sidebarState` and `localStorage`.
   - Full keyboard accessibility and ARIA state handling.
3. **Reader Layout Styles (`docs-astro/src/styles/reader-layout.css`)**:
   - Collapsed rail mode (`3.5rem`), hover/focus overlay expansion (`18.75rem`).
   - Touch devices isolated with `@media (hover: none)`.
   - Motion safety with `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
