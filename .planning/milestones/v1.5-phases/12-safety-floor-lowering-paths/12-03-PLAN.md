<objective>
Close the MCP preference lowering path (FLOOR-03, roadmap SC-3: "refused").

Purpose: `vox_preference_set` is reachable from any MCP client, the chat agent loop, and the HTTP gateway's default allowed tools. It writes to the DB and then live-mutates `socrates_gate_enforce`, `attention_enabled`, `attention_budget_ms` and `attention_alert_threshold` with no key validation (research Pitfall 3). CONTEXT classes these writes as policy grants. Refusal is the stricter form, and the roadmap and VALIDATION row both say "refused". Humans keep their paths: the GUI settings writing Vox.toml, and the `vox attention` CLI. Neither goes through this MCP tool.

Output: a key-prefix guard at the top of `preference_set`; the now-unreachable live-mutation branches are removed.
</objective>
