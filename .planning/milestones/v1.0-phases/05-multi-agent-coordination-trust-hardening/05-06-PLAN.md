<objective>
Close the lock lifecycle: every terminal path of a lock-holding task releases its lock and wakes the
waiters, and expired leases are purged lazily (D-06, D-08, D-13).

Purpose: without release-on-completion/failure a lock is held for its full 30-minute lease and
waiters stay parked (05-RESEARCH Pitfalls 3/4); without the sweep, expired entries accumulate and
over-report in `len()`/`snapshot()` (Pattern 2). This finishes MESH-01's "correctly handling lease
expiration and contention".

Output: three commits (success/failure release; cancellation release; lazy sweep) and mutation
proofs, plus the MESH-01 crate gates.
</objective>
