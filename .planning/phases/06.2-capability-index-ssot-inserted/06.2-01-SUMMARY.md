# Phase 06.2 Plan 01: Capability Index Tracer Summary

**Catalog-derived capability index (996 cli + 340 mcp_tool rows) with a schema, a `vox ci capability-index` generator wired into ssot-drift and ssot-regen, and an include_str!-embedded loader in vox-capability-registry.**

## Accomplishments

- `contracts/capabilities/capability-index.v1.schema.json` (draft 2020-12, `additionalProperties: false`, every key required) and the generated `capability-index.v1.json` (about 1.3 MB, marked `linguist-generated -diff`).
- `vox-capability-registry::index`: the v1 types with the contract field order, `CapabilityIndex::row`, and `bundled_index()` over a `OnceLock`.
- `vox ci capability-index [--write]`: `inputs::load` (committed catalog only, feature-independent), pure `join::build_index`, schema validation, `write_or_verify_text`. Rows are ids `cli.<path>` / `mcp.<tool>` from the registry helpers, sorted by (kind, id), no timestamps; duplicate ids and ops with no projection fail naming the id; paired ops list each other in `equivalents`; summaries are single-line and control-free (T-06.2-01).
- Gate wiring: `ds!("capability_index", ...)` right after `capability_sync` in `run_ssot_drift`, regen step `m` in `scripts/ssot-regen.vox`, catalog op `ci.capability-index`, cli.md entry, `.gitattributes` line.
