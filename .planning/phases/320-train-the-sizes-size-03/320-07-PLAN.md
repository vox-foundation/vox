**Facts the procedure relies on.**
- `serde_json` writes a NaN or infinite `f64` as `null`, so a NaN loss appears in `telemetry.jsonl` as `payload.loss == null`.
- Without `--max-runtime-secs` a local run trains its full epoch count; with it, Plan 02's cap stops at the next optimizer boundary and `run_summary.json` says `capped: true`; the Plan 03 record then says `capped`.
- A paused or interrupted run leaves `checkpoint_state.json`; `--resume <dir>` with the same `--model` continues it. The base check refuses any other base.
- The lane prefix is added by the Phase 313 encoder on every row; S0 re-ran its tests on the built code.

**Run procedure R(S)** (S fits; from `$R`; `D=mens/runs/qwen3-$S`, size directory names lowercase, for example `qwen3-0.6b`, `qwen3-14b`):
1. Done already: skip when `$D/train-record.json` has `status` `trained` or `capped` and `$D/candle_qlora_adapter.safetensors` exists.
2. Running already: when `pgrep -f "^$ML mens train .*$D"` finds a trainer, wait for it instead of starting another.
3. Cap and budget: for 0.6B, 1.7B, 4B, 8B no cap (D-07 full pass); for 14B and 32B `CAP=14400` (D-07). Safety timeout `T` = the larger of 7200 and twice `projected_full_secs` for uncapped sizes, `CAP + 7200` for capped ones.
4. Start or resume, in the background: `mkdir -p $D && timeout --signal=INT --kill-after=900s ${T}s $ML mens train $COMMON --model Qwen3-$S --output-dir $D ${CAP:+--max-runtime-secs $CAP} <RESUME> >> $D/train.log 2>&1; echo $? > $E/s4-$S.exit`. `<RESUME>` is `--resume $D` when `$D/checkpoint_state.json` exists (then, for a capped size, `CAP` becomes 14400 minus the previous record's `train_wall_secs`, at least 600); otherwise it is empty. Never pass `--force-restart` here.
5. Interval checks: about every tenth of `projected_full_secs` (at least 10 minutes; every 30 minutes for capped runs), use the harness's wait or monitor facility, then run one command: the last `step` row's `optimizer_step`, `progress_fraction` and `loss`, plus the divergence test, which fires when any `step` row has a null loss, or when there are at least 60 step rows and the mean loss of the last 20 exceeds the mean of the first 20. No shell polling loops.
6. Divergence: send `kill -INT` once to the trainer PID (step 2's `pgrep`), wait for it to exit, then set the record's `status` to `not_trained` and its `reason` to `diverged: <the test that fired and its numbers>` with `jq`, delete nothing, and continue with the next size.
7. After exit, check: exit 0; record `status` `trained` (uncapped) or `capped`/`trained` (capped); `$D/candle_qlora_adapter.safetensors` exists; `jq -r .base_model $D/adapter_manifest.json` contains the pin; `$D/run_summary.json` `base_model` is `Qwen/Qwen3-<S>@<pin>`; loss falls (first-quarter mean above last-quarter mean over the step rows, at least 8 rows); `peak_mb` above 0. Any failure other than divergence or out of memory stops the plan with its message (fix the code first, D-05). An out-of-memory failure on a size its probe said fits is recorded as `not_trained` with the measurement and reported as a finding (the probe missed the peak), and the climb rule of D-06 applies to the sizes above it.
8. Evidence: copy the record to `$E/s4-record-$S.json`, `tail -200 $D/train.log` to `$E/s4-$S-log.txt`, and write `$E/s4-loss-$S.json` as `{n, first_quarter_mean, last_quarter_mean, last_loss, optimizer_steps}`.
