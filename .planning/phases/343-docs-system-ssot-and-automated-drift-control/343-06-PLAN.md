Flag docs pages that nothing links to, and drive the reader-facing ones to zero (or to a user-authorized shrink-only baseline).

Purpose: DOCS-DRIFT-04 part 2. RESEARCH F4 measured ~229 non-archive pages with zero inbound links, ~48 of them reader-facing. Output: the fixed graph builder, the `docs_orphan_pages` stage, the decision applied, SSOT §4/§6 and ledger updates.
</objective>
