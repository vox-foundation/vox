## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| catalog.toml -> compiled binary | catalog.toml is embedded via `include_str!` and parsed at build time into `CatalogFile`, so its content becomes data inside the shipped binary. A malformed or semantically wrong entry here reaches every install. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-04 | Tampering | `crates/vox-plugin-catalog/catalog.toml` | medium | mitigate | The edit is asserted comment-only by a `git diff ... \| grep '^\+[^+]' \| grep -v '^\+#'` acceptance check, so an added or altered `[[plugin]]` entry — which would change what the binary offers to install, including `default-source` repo URLs and `sha256` pins — fails the task rather than shipping. |
