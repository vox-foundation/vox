# Phase 396 Plan 02: Wave 1D — Second-Tier Keystone & High-Churn Compile-Time Optimization Across 11 Crates

## Objective
Apply `#[inline(never)]` compilation firewalls and domain boundary outlinings across the 11 second-tier keystone and high-churn crates identified in `contracts/ci/crate-build-map.v1.json` to prevent LLVM/rustc from inlining massive AST/HIR state machines, async handlers, and serde expansions across crate boundaries.
