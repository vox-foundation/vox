# Phase 384 — UI Design Contract (`384-UI-SPEC.md`)
## Diegetic Roman Simulator Art, Animated Aqueduct Watercourses, Living Citizen Crowds & Zero-Waste Layout Overhaul

>Generated by `/gsd-ui-phase` (gsd-ui-researcher + gsd-ui-checker verified) for Milestone `v6.5` Phase 384.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Visual Diagnosis

Computer-vision inspection of the live Urbs Civilization Simulator (`LudusSandbox.tsx`, `worldRenderer.ts`, `hydraulicNetwork.ts`, `GamifyView.tsx`) and Aqueduct Pipeline Cascade (`HarnessRiverPanel.tsx`, `riverCanvasRenderer.ts`) across `1280×800`, `1440×900`, `1920×1080`, and docked multi-panel layouts identified four structural and visual defects:

1. **Cropped & Obscured Simulator Artwork (`LudusSandbox.tsx`, `HarnessRiverPanel.tsx`)**:
   - `object-cover` aspect-ratio clipping sliced off the top 20% of the Acropolis Temple pediment and Colosseum crown, and sliced off the bottom 20% of the Portus Harbor quays, ships, and lighthouse—especially in wide or docked containers.
   - Monument callout pills rendered tiny `24×24px` cropped square thumbnails inside large opaque cream boxes that covered the painted monuments underneath.
   - In docked mode (`ChatSurface` 2D grid), floating HUD overlays covered over 70% of the simulator viewport.
2. **Pasted-Over Straight Vector Lines Across Rooftops (`worldRenderer.ts`, `hydraulicNetwork.ts`, `LudusSandbox.tsx`)**:
   - `drawRomanHydraulicInfrastructure()` drew 7.4px dark-brown/red/yellow straight lines between procedural grid coordinates (`worldPx`) that did not match the painted diorama buildings, slashing diagonally across the Colosseum arena, Pantheon dome, and temple roofs.
   - `LudusSandbox.tsx` simultaneously drew 6 dashed SVG Bézier lines from `(21%, 56%)` across the sky and rooftops.
   - `riverCanvasRenderer.ts` drew 3 translucent rectangles (`ctx.strokeRect`) and crossing lines on top of a darkly glazed `164px` cropped strip of `aqueduct-sluice-cascade-bg.jpg`.
3. **Lack of Diegetic Environmental Animation (Water & Crowds)**:
   - Water in the aqueducts, cascades, canals, and harbor was static pixels; connections were represented as pasted-over graph edges instead of water flowing *inside* the stone aqueduct channels and crowds walking *along* the stone streets.
4. **Vertical & Horizontal Whitespace Waste (`GamifyView.tsx`, `HarnessRiverPanel.tsx`, `LudusSandbox.tsx`)**:
   - `GamifyView.tsx` stacked an outer header, a 2-row frieze card, the canvas, and a separate bottom accordion bar, while `LudusSandbox.tsx` stacked two separate bottom HUD rows (`bottom-[64px]` action dock + `bottom-1.5` gauge panels), eating 140px+ of vertical height.
   - `HarnessRiverPanel.tsx` used a separate full-width bar just for `Window: Current Session` and `Verification Gate`, compressing the Aqueduct Cascade canvas to `164px`.

## 2. Diegetic Visual Architecture Contract

### 2.1 Zero Pasted-Over Rooftop Lines → Diegetic Animated Watercourses
- **Remove Rooftop Graph Lines**:
  - Disable arbitrary procedural grid-to-grid `drawConduitTrack` / `strokeSegment` lines in `worldRenderer.ts` when composited over the painted diorama (`urbs-white-gold-citadel-bg.jpg`), and remove the sky-crossing `<svg>` dashed lines in `LudusSandbox.tsx`.
- **Diegetic Aqueduct, Waterfall Cascade, Forum Canal & Harbor Water Shader (`DiegeticWatercourses`)**:
  - Align multi-waypoint curved water channels directly to the stone architecture in the panorama:
    1. **Upper Acropolis & Aqua Marcia Aqueduct Trough**: Flows along the elevated stone arches from the western hills (`4%, 54%` → `18%, 47%` → `33%, 41%`) into the central **Nymphaeum / Castellum Basin** (`44%, 40%`), with a northern branch feeding the **GUI Pantheon & Colosseum** (`44%, 40%` → `56%, 34%` → `72%, 33%`).
    2. **Central Nymphaeum Waterfall & Via Sacra Stone Canal**: Cascades down the stepped marble waterfall (`44%, 40%` → `48%, 51%`) and flows along the recessed stone canal through the Forum (`48%, 51%` → `58%, 60%` → `69%, 69%`) into the **Portus Harbor Basin** (`76%, 76%` → `90%, 82%`).
    3. **Western Praetorium Sluice Branch**: Flows from the Aqueduct arcade (`22%, 49%` → `27%, 60%` → `42%, 67%` → `64%, 73%`) connecting the `Compiler`, `Agent Orchestrator`, `Database`, and `Compute Mesh` districts into the harbor.
  - Render animated turquoise/aquamarine water ribbons, sunlit gold caustic ripples, white waterfall foam crests at drop-basins, and bobbing harbor waves that speed up and glow emerald/gold when tasks execute.

### 2.2 Living Roman Citizen, Legionary & Caravan Crowds (`DiegeticCrowdSimulation`)
- **Street-Aligned Waypoint Network**:
  - Replace invisible grid-tile clamping with 18–24 animated diegetic citizens, senators, torch-bearing scholars, legionary patrols, and harbor merchant galleys following the actual painted cobblestone roads (`Via Sacra`, `Clivus Capitolinus`, `Forum Colonnade`, `Colosseum Esplanade`, `Portus Quay`) and harbor basin.
  - Render animated walking strides, fluttering cloaks, carried scrolls/vexilla, and soft ground shadows; scale crowd speed and density along active crate routes when orchestrator tasks or tool calls fire.

### 2.3 Uncropped Safe-Frame Panorama & Compact Diegetic Monument Signposts
- **Safe-Frame Panorama Rendering**:
  - Compose `urbs-white-gold-citadel-bg.jpg` with full-frame architectural safe margins (complete Acropolis pediment & Colosseum crown at top; complete Portus Harbor quays, triremes, and lighthouse at bottom) and render with aspect-aware framing so neither standalone nor docked viewports crop out the landmarks.
- **Diegetic Monument Frieze Signposts**:
  - Replace bulky opaque white pill cards and cropped `24×24px` square thumbnails with compact, chiseled marble-and-gold architectural nameplates (`px-2 py-0.5`) anchored cleanly at each monument's pediment/terrace, while showing the full uncropped monument painting inside `RomanTooltip`.

### 2.4 Zero-Waste Perimeter HUD Consolidation
- **`GamifyView.tsx`**:
  - Consolidate the frieze header, subtab switcher, and quests/notices quick-jump into a single compact row (`py-1.5`), expanding the simulator viewport to `h-[calc(100vh-148px)] min-h-[540px]`.
- **`LudusSandbox.tsx`**:
  - Merge the two stacked bottom bars (`bottom-[64px]` Semiotic Layer & Action Dock + `bottom-1.5` Budget/Energy/Speed/Zoom bar) into a unified single-tier bottom perimeter dock (`bottom-2`) in standalone mode, and an ultra-slim 1-row micro-bar in `embedded` mode.
- **`HarnessRiverPanel.tsx` & `riverCanvasRenderer.ts`**:
  - Merge the `Window` selector and `Verification Gate` badge into the Aqueduct header bar, increase the Aqueduct Cascade canvas height to `220px–260px`, and render diegetic animated water waves, sluice foam, and golden token ingots *inside* the 3 painted stone aqueduct troughs (`Cursus I–III`) without dark brown glazes or pasted-over rectangles.
