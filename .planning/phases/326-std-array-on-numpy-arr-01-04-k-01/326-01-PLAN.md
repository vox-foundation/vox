# Phase 326 Plan 01: Z-score tracer through one materializer

<objective>
Ship one production path: `examples/golden/py_array_stats.vox` computes the z-score of `[1.0, 3.0]` with `correction=0`, asserts `[-1.0, 1.0]`, and crosses the Phase 325 worker once, at `to_list` (ARR-01, D-01 subset, D-02, D-03).

Output: the golden, the seven yaml rows that expression needs, `Ty::Array`, `VoxValue::ArrayPlan`, and the worker handlers for those seven names.

What is verifiable before Phase 325 lands: the pure-plan unit test (`array_plan_zscore_is_pure`) and `vox check` of plan methods that do not materialize. </objective>
