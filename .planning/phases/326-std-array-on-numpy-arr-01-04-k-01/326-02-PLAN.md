# Phase 326 Plan 02: Make the other five ops callable

<objective>
Turn `add`, `mul`, `sort`, `item`, and `shape` into callable plans or materializers, and lock their on-disk expected objects to the behavior cases (ARR-01, D-01, D-02, D-03).

Purpose: a yaml row and a worker def are not an op. sort must build a plan. item and shape must call `execute_plan` and require the python capability.
Output: five yaml rows, five handlers, the plan and materializer wiring, and `on_disk_expected_matches_behavior_cases`.

Compile-error coverage for mixed dtypes and `nulls=` is plan 07. `vox ci python-bridge-parity` is plan 08. Do not add a per-op `@test`.
</objective>

No new HirType. No new lexer keyword. No new crate. No crate-edge exception.
