Inspection method, for budget: find the handler via the clap variant's `cli_dispatch` arm (`vox graph query "<verb> handler"` or a scoped `rg` under crates/vox-cli/src/commands/ and the owning crate). Then grep the handler body and its direct callees for write signals rather than reading whole files:
- `fs::write`, `create_dir`, `remove_`, `rename`, `File::create`, `OpenOptions`
- `persist`, `save`, `insert`, `upsert`, `execute(`, `INSERT`, `UPDATE`, `DELETE`
- `Command::new`, `spawn(`
- `.post(`, `.put(`, `.delete(`
- `--fix`, `--write`, `--apply` flags

`vox ci operations-verify` printed "inventory report updated", which suggests `ci … verify` verbs may write reports. Check each one.

Per-verb budget (bounds each group task; checker W3): at most two scoped searches to locate the handler and grep its body plus direct callees for the write signals above, and at most one targeted read of ≤60 lines. If the handler or its effect is not established within that budget, stop on that verb and record `null` with the reason "not verified within budget: <what is missing>". Null is an allowed, safe outcome: the UI treats null as side-effecting (D-01) and the ratchet keeps it visible. Never upgrade a verb to read_only or none on partial evidence.
